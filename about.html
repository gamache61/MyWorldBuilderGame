WORLD BUILDER

ABOUT / HOW TO PLAY

THE STORY

It begins on Earth. You build the explorer ship the Seeker and fly it into deep space, where it finds The Keeper — an ancient being that asks the people of Earth to build a new world of peace on the green planet Unity.

Then you build the cargo ship the Aegis Ark, and chapter by chapter you build everything the new world needs: a sky station, cities, a council hall, farms, mines, schools, homes and more. Between projects, the Unity Council meets to decide what to build next — and sometimes the deciding vote is yours.

CHAPTERS

  • The story is played in order, one chapter at a time. Your place is saved, so CONTINUE on the front page takes you back to where you were.
  • Every build in a chapter is one day in the story, with day and night, sunrise and sunset.
  • When a chapter is finished it is marked COMPLETE and you can go straight on to the next one.

HOW TO PLAY

  • The NEXT STEP at the top middle of the screen always tells you what to do next — click its button or press its key.
  • All the controls are in the drop-down menu, grouped by category (press M to open or close it). Each control shows its key.
  • The Aegis Ark brings the parts from the warehouse on Earth. Load the cargo bay, warp to the build site, and unload the parts with the crane or the transporter.
  • In the council meetings, listen to the council and cast your vote when they ask you.
